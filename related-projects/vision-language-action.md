# Vision-Language-Action

This category collects forked and related open-source projects relevant to vision-language-action modeling and embodied intelligence for robotic haircutting research.

- [lingbot-vla](https://github.com/Dai0731csc/lingbot-vla): forked reference project related to vision-language-action research, relevant to task interpretation and higher-level robotic intelligence.
- [openvla](https://github.com/Dai0731csc/openvla): forked open-source VLA baseline relevant to intelligent robotic manipulation and embodied AI directions connected to robotic haircutting.
