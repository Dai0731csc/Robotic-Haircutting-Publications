# Vision-Language-Action

This category collects forked and related open-source projects on vision-language-action modeling and embodied intelligence that inform robotic haircutting research.

- [lingbot-vla](https://github.com/Dai0731csc/lingbot-vla): a forked reference project related to vision-language-action research. It is useful for task interpretation and higher-level robotic intelligence.
- [openvla](https://github.com/Dai0731csc/openvla): a forked open-source VLA baseline useful for intelligent robotic manipulation and embodied AI directions connected to robotic haircutting.
