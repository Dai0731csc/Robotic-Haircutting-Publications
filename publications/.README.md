# Robotic Haircutting Publications

This directory collects publications and related research materials on robotic haircutting.

The work represented here is associated with the Biomimetics and Intelligent Systems Group (BISG) at the University of Oulu. It spans robotics, intelligent control, teleoperation, perception, planning, and human-robot interaction.

The publications collected here focus on the development of safe, precise, and human-centered robotic systems for personal-care tasks. Topics include robotic perception, hair and head modeling, task planning, teleoperation, motion control, safety constraints, and human-robot interaction in close proximity to the human head.
